# <i class="fas fa-user section-icon"></i> About Me

I am a PhD student in Electronic Information at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I began my doctoral studies in 2024 under the supervision of Prof. [Wanli Ouyang](https://wlouyang.github.io/). I am currently collaborating with Prof. [Tao Gui](https://guitaowufeng.github.io/) on the development of [foundation models](https://atria-asi.ai/). Prior to my PhD, I earned my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology ([rank 1/196](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/YOJcpipRe0TgYSeJgOCwCA#:~:text=%E4%BB%B2%E8%8B%B1%E4%B9%A6%E9%99%A2-,%E8%AE%A1%E7%AE%97%E6%9C%BA004%E7%8F%AD%C2%A0%E5%BE%90%E6%9C%9B%E7%80%9A,-%F0%9F%94%B5%20%E8%BF%9E%E7%BB%AD%E4%B8%A4%E5%B9%B4)) from Xi’an Jiaotong University.

My research interests span large language models, multi-agent systems, and generative AI, with a current focus on advancing the frontier of agentic coding through Recursive Self-Improvement. Previously, I worked on enhancing the reasoning capabilities of AI models in complex problem-solving scenarios, including [deep research](https://openai.com/index/introducing-deep-research/) (try my [**Deep Research🔎**](https://dr-sigma-wheat.vercel.app/)) and automated scientific discovery (auto research). You can download my [[CV](/pdfs/WanghanXu.pdf)] here.

If you are interested in agentic coding, feel free to contact me at [xu_wanghan@sjtu.edu.cn](mailto:xu_wanghan@sjtu.edu.cn).

> By the way, you can ask me anything through this [**Bot🤖**](https://chat-xi-three-29.vercel.app/). And if you ever want my WeChat ID📱… well, maybe this [**Bot🤖**](https://chat-xi-three-29.vercel.app/) can tell you — if you’re smart enough to figure it out. 😉 Just kidding! Good luck!
